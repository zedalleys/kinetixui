## 4. SwiftUI — the registration question, answered by execution

The brief asks whether the missing registration is #636's **only** CI blocker. **It is not.** Measured
in two probes on the composed candidate, each applied, run, and restored:

**Probe 1 — register SwiftUI as a `motion.json` consumer.** `check:native-motion` reports the *same
single problem, unchanged*:

> ✗ SwiftUI/KinetixSelectionIndicator.swift: animates, but is in no motion **family** and is not listed
> in KNOWN_GAPS.

So the register the ledger cares about is **not** the contract's `consumers` list — it is the
`families` map in `scripts/native-motion-spec.mjs`. Registering in the contract alone changes nothing.

**Probe 2 — add `SwiftUI.families.selectionIndicator`** pointing at #636's CI-run suite. One problem
becomes **two**:

> ✗ SwiftUI: no test in `packages/ui-swiftui/Tests/KinetixUITests/SelectionIndicatorTests.swift` is
> NAMED for the forward direction. … Found: "test Forward And Backward Travel Require Stable Geometry",
> "test Reduced Motion Removes Travel Even When Layout Is Stable"
>
> ✗ SwiftUI: no test … is NAMED for the reverse direction. [same two names]

`DIRECTION_TERMS.selectionIndicator` requires a test **name** carrying `earlier → later` for forward and
`later → earlier` for reverse. #636's two names satisfy neither, and under #624's distinctness clause one
name could not carry both anyway. **Renaming those tests is content on #636's own branch**, which this
phase must not modify.

**Resolution taken: a declared `KNOWN_GAPS` entry**, not a registration and not a disabled ledger. Its
comment records the measurement above and the exact condition for removal — *"Remove this line when
that suite names its two directions — then the family entry is the whole change."* Declared gaps go
9 → 10, and `check:native-motion` then passes: **37 components across 3 platforms**.

**Also recorded honestly:** `swift` is **not available in this container**, so #636's implementation
and its `swift test` suite were **not executed here**. The `Acceptance/Selection` harness
(`MotionHost.xcodeproj`, `record-selection.sh`, `measure-selection.py`) is hand-run on a Mac and is
**not** run by any workflow; `native-swiftui.yml` runs `swift test`, which does cover the unit suite —
on GitHub, not here.
