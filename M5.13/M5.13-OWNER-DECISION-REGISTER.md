## D1 — The missing-changeset hole: changesets now, or land after the release?

**Measured.** A published package's *source* can change with no changeset and every release gate stays
green. The gates ask whether a **pending changeset** is described in the curated note; they never ask
whether a source change has one. Live instances, each re-verified from its own merge-base with `main`:
**#640, #641, #642, #631, #636** — all five change `packages/ui/**`, `packages/ui-angular/**` or
`packages/ui-swiftui/**` and carry no changeset.

**What it costs each way.** Adding changesets makes them release-note-bearing, which forces either an
extension of `release/notes/0.28.0.json`'s `coverage` plus a regenerated Version PR, or a 0.28.1 note.
Landing them after the release costs nothing now and delays the accessibility fixes by a release.

**If deferred:** whichever of those five merges first ships an accessibility or motion behaviour change
inside 0.28.0 / 0.26.3 with no changelog line and no release-note sentence — silently, with CI green.

**Recommendation:** land them *after* 0.28.0, each with a changeset. It keeps the release candidate
stable and the paperwork honest, at the cost of one release cycle.

`XCUIVoiceOverService`. `grep -rn "Acceptance/Sheet"` outside that directory returns nothing, and
`verification.json` has **no Sheet entry at all**.

**Consequence:** delete `.accessibilityAddTraits(.isModal)` or the `onDisappear` guard and **nothing in
CI goes red**.

**If deferred:** the repair ships with no regression protection, and the next refactor can undo it
silently.

**Recommendation:** add a `macos-latest` job running
`xcodebuild test -scheme MotionHost -destination 'platform=iOS Simulator,…'`. Whether VoiceOver
automation works on a hosted runner is the open question; if it does not, record explicitly that the
suite is owner-run and unguarded between runs rather than leaving it implied.
