Planned from the CAT-1 audit ([`../audits/CAT-1-CATALOGUE-AUDIT.md`](../audits/CAT-1-CATALOGUE-AUDIT.md)) at `main`
`984792b` (2026-10-18). Defects are in [`../../UX-QUALITY-BACKLOG.md`](../../UX-QUALITY-BACKLOG.md).

or QER, or touches IoT or release automation.

## Sequencing, and what does not have to wait

The architecture work (CAT-2) and the visible defects (CAT-3) are **independent**. Thumbnail quality does not need
the taxonomy migration: a redrawn `FooterMock` or `TableMock` is a change to one file. So CAT-3 should not queue
behind CAT-2.

**Deliverable independently, in any order, before or alongside CAT-2:**

## CAT-2 — Canonical metadata, taxonomy and filtering

**Objective.** Give functional category a canonical home, delete the duplicate maps, and separate category from
technology, so every later slice reads one source.

**Scope.**
- `category` in `components.manifest.json` for all 109 entries, with the eight-value enum from audit §4.
- `kind` (`component` / `recipe`), seeded from the existing "not a component" note derivation.
- `facets` (`composed`, `utility`) for `address-fields`, `form`, `direction-provider`.
- `COMPONENT_CATEGORY` (site.ts) and `GROUP` (gen-stories.mjs) derived from the manifest; both literals deleted.
- The silent `?? "Data Display"` fallback removed.
- `check:manifest` extended: every entry categorised, every category in the enum, no empty category, no entry
  relying on a fallback.
- The card pill shows the category; technology stays on the detail page.
- `?cat=` keeps accepting the three renamed values (`Form Inputs`, `Controls & Actions`, `Feedback`) so shared
  links resolve.

**Out of scope.** Moving any entry between Components and Blocks; renaming or removing any public API; a technology
filter.

**Dependencies.** None.

**Affected files.** `components.manifest.json`, `scripts/gen-manifest.mjs`, `scripts/gen-stories.mjs`,
`apps/web/src/lib/site.ts`, `apps/web/src/lib/component-registry.ts`,
`apps/web/src/components/component-gallery.tsx`, `apps/web/src/components/component-gallery.test.tsx`,
story files regenerated by `gen:stories`.

**Acceptance criteria.**
1. Exactly one place in the repository maps a slug to a category, and it is the manifest.
2. `pnpm check:manifest && pnpm check:stories` pass; the generator chain reaches a fixed point with no diff.
3. Every card's pill contains a value from the enum; no card shows a technology there.
4. `/components?cat=Form%20Inputs` still filters (redirect or alias to `Forms & Inputs`).
5. 25 entries change category, three categories are renamed, no entry is uncategorised, no category is empty.

**Test strategy.** Unit tests for the alias resolution and the derivation; `check:manifest` negative controls
(missing category, invalid category, duplicate slug); a rendered check over all 109 cards; the existing
`component-gallery.test.tsx` extended.

**Risks.** The Storybook `GROUP` feeds the accessibility suites' subject list, so a derivation bug could silently
change what axe runs over — assert the generated subject list is unchanged except for the two slugs the maps
currently disagree on. Category renames appear in the sidebar, the filter, Storybook titles and search; the fixed
point check is what catches a missed surface.

**Complexity.** Medium. One PR.

## CAT-3 — Component and block thumbnail quality

**Objective.** Make every gallery thumbnail recognisable at card size, in every environment, without faking what a
component is.

**Scope.**
- `previewFraming` per entry (audit §7: `fit`, `scale`, `align`, `stage`, `crop`), canonical in the manifest,
  consumed by the gallery.
- Fix the 11 clipped thumbnails (003, 004, 005, and `result-state`, `carousel`, `address-fields`, `file-upload`,
  `calendar`, `color-picker`, `time-slot-picker`, `virtual-list`).
- Fix the 21 near-empty thumbnails (001).
- Redraw the skeleton mocks with real content (006, 007).
- Differentiate the five near-identical pairs (008).
- Raise rendered text above the legibility floor (018), including inside scroll containers.
- Make the frame survive 200% text (017) without `overflow: hidden` cropping.
- Top-align and fade a too-tall preview rather than cropping it symmetrically.

**Out of scope.** Changing any component's own source to suit its preview; replacing a working demo with a
skeleton; block showcases (CAT-5).

**Dependencies.** The quick fixes in the table above need nothing. The framing record is cleaner after CAT-2 (same
schema work, same gate), so run CAT-2 first if both are queued — but a mock redraw must not wait for it.

**Affected files.** `apps/web/src/components/component-thumbnails.tsx`,
`apps/web/src/components/component-gallery.tsx`, `components.manifest.json`, demo registry entries where a demo is
authored for card size.

**Acceptance criteria.**
1. No thumbnail has content clipped by its frame at 320/390/768/1280 in light, dark, RTL, forced colors and
   reduced motion, and the 200% text count is a recorded, shrinking number rather than 50+.
2. Every thumbnail's content covers at least an agreed minimum of its frame, exceptions recorded with reasons.
3. No rendered text in a thumbnail is below the agreed floor.
4. Any two thumbnails differ by more than an agreed pixel share, exceptions recorded.
5. Mocks still contain nothing focusable, no heading, no role or name (existing tests keep passing), and the
   footer/breadcrumb/pagination hydration constraint still holds — no anchors inside a card's link.

**Test strategy.** `scripts/catalogue-preview-audit.mjs` becomes the gate's engine in CAT-6; in CAT-3 it is run
before and after and the numbers recorded in the PR. Negative control per fix: restore the old mock or the old
frame, show the measurement regress.

**Risks.** A mock that looks better but misrepresents the component (the brief's "do not imply unsupported
functionality"): every redrawn mock must be checked against the real component's demo. Scaling to fit can push
text below the legibility floor — the two criteria must be checked together.

**Complexity.** High (the largest visible-surface slice). Suggest two PRs: mocks and pairs first, then the framing
record with the clipped and near-empty entries.

## CAT-4 — Detail preview UX, toolbar spacing and state documentation

**Objective.** Make the component detail page's preview panel proportionate, consistently inset, and able to show
the states a component actually has.

**Scope.**
- Per-entry stage height, from the CAT-3 framing record, ending the universal `min-h-[350px]` (009).
- One inset scale across the panel's three regions (010, 011).
- The state vocabulary and the SUPPORTED / TESTED / DOCUMENTED / NOT APPLICABLE / NOT IMPLEMENTED ladder
  (015), recorded per entry, with TESTED reusing `scripts/evidence-levels.mjs`.
- A generated **States** section on the detail page, from the declared states.
- Record the DataTable / DataGrid data-state gap as a product question; do not implement component states here.

**Out of scope.** State badges on gallery cards; adding states to components; changing the evidence tiers'
meaning.

**Dependencies.** The stage height shares CAT-3's framing record. The inset fix depends on nothing.

**Affected files.** `apps/web/src/components/preview-environment.tsx`,
`apps/web/src/components/component-preview.tsx`, `components.manifest.json` (state declarations), a new generator
or an extension of `gen:docs`, `apps/web/src/app/docs/components/*/page.mdx` where generated.

**Acceptance criteria.**
1. No single-control preview leaves more than an agreed share of its stage empty; 200% text still fits.
2. The panel's regions share one start inset, in LTR and RTL, at all four viewports.
3. Every state claimed SUPPORTED corresponds to a real variant key or prop; nothing is SUPPORTED because a tag
   says so.
4. Every state claimed DOCUMENTED has a story or a doc example that renders it.
5. `Chart`'s loading, empty and error states are discoverable from its page.

**Test strategy.** Rendered measurement for the panel; a data gate for the state claims (parse the API, compare
with the declaration, fail on a claim with no backing); negative control — declare a state the component does not
implement, assert the gate fails.

**Risks.** The state declaration is a new per-entry data surface and could become a second verification system.
Keeping TESTED derived from the existing evidence machinery is the guard; review the schema with that in mind.

**Complexity.** Medium-high. Two PRs: panel geometry, then state vocabulary and the States section.

## CAT-5 — Business-oriented block discovery

**Objective.** Let a developer find a block by the job it does.

**Scope.**
- Map all 21 blocks to a capability in `docs/business-capabilities.json`, adding `account-settings` and
  `content-marketing` (audit §5).
- Make capability the primary axis on `/blocks`; keep `category` in the manifest and `?cat=` working.
- Workflow stays a secondary facet; industry stays out of the UI until more blocks carry one.
- Stage-width hint per block (020).
- Record the workflow gaps (checkout has blocks at 2 of 6 stages; `booking-scheduling` and `workflow` have none)
  as proposals with evidence, without inventing blocks.

**Out of scope.** New blocks; duplicating a block per industry; a capability field in `blocks.manifest.json`.

**Dependencies.** None on CAT-2, though the two share a review of how taxonomy is declared.

**Affected files.** `docs/business-capabilities.json`, `scripts/business-discovery.mjs`,
`apps/web/src/app/blocks/blocks-gallery.tsx`, `apps/web/src/lib/block-filters.ts`,
`apps/web/src/components/showcase.tsx`, `blocks.manifest.json` (framing hint only).

**Acceptance criteria.**
1. Every published block has exactly one primary capability; every offered capability has at least one block.
2. `?cat=` links still resolve; `?capability=` is the primary filter.
3. `pnpm check:capabilities` passes and `discovery-index.json` is not stale.
4. No block covers less than an agreed share of its stage, exceptions recorded.

**Test strategy.** Extend `block-filters.test.ts` for the new primary axis; a gate for the one-capability rule and
the no-empty-category rule; rendered measurement for the stages.

**Risks.** Two new capabilities enter a vocabulary shared with `/capabilities` and the component facets, so they
change counts on pages beyond `/blocks` — check every surface that reads the index.

**Complexity.** Medium. One PR.

## CAT-6 — Catalogue regression and quality gates

**Objective.** Turn the numbers CAT-1 measured into gates, so none of this can regress silently.

**Scope.** One catalogue gate with a thresholds file, built on `scripts/catalogue-preview-audit.mjs`:

| Check | Source finding |
|---|---|
| Every entry has a valid category; no fallback; no empty category | 019 |
| Every block has exactly one capability; no empty capability | 014 |
| No orphaned entry: manifest ↔ `componentDocs` ↔ demo registry ↔ doc page all agree | §3.1 |
| No duplicate slug; every detail link resolves | brief §10 |
| Manifest ↔ evidence consistency across every platform cell | 016 |
| No state claimed without a backing variant or prop | 015 |
| Minimum thumbnail ink and content-to-frame fill | 001 |
| No thumbnail content clipped by its frame, at default and 200% text | 003, 004, 017 |
| Minimum rendered text size in a thumbnail, including inside scroll containers | 018 |
| Minimum pixel difference between any two thumbnails | 007, 008 |
| Maximum empty share of a detail stage and a block stage | 009, 020 |
| Minimum inset between a panel border and the controls inside it | 010, 011 |
| No horizontal page overflow on `/components` or `/blocks` in any environment | baseline: 0 today |

**Out of scope.** Committed screenshot baselines (the audit explains why relations beat snapshots on a shared
runner); new analytics.

**Dependencies.** Each check lands with or after the slice that makes it pass, so the gate is never added red.

**Affected files.** `scripts/catalogue-preview-audit.mjs` (gains assertions and a thresholds file),
`package.json` (`check:catalogue`), a CI workflow, `docs/audits/CAT-1-CATALOGUE-AUDIT.md` (status note).

**Acceptance criteria.**
1. The gate runs in CI on a built site, at 320/390/768/1280, light / dark / RTL / 200% text, and fails the build.
2. Every threshold is recorded with the measurement it came from, and every exception carries a reason.
3. A negative control exists per check and is run: an invalid category, a removed preview, a broken detail link, a
   platform marked available without evidence, a state declared that the component cannot show, and a
   deliberately clipped preview each make the right check fail — restored afterwards, never committed broken.

**Test strategy.** The negative controls are the test strategy. Keep the thresholds at the measured values rather
than at aspirational ones, so the gate's first run is green and tightening is a deliberate commit.

**Risks.** A browser gate is slow and can flake; scope it to `/components`, `/blocks` and a representative set of
detail pages rather than all 109 pages, and follow the repository's existing pattern of measuring relations rather
than diffing pixels. CAT-1 found this script takes about four minutes for the full matrix on this container.

**Complexity.** Medium. One PR, landing last.
