# 30 / 60 / 90-day roadmap

Outcomes are directional. There is no baseline yet, so any specific number here
would be invented — and inventing numbers is the one thing this project's
positioning cannot survive.

## Days 1–30 — Foundation and first campaigns

**Theme:** establish the position with people who can judge it.

- Run the 30-day calendar (2 articles, ~10 LinkedIn, ~11 X, 2 Reddit, 2 GitHub)
- Execute **Campaign 1 — "Cross-platform design systems should prove parity"**
- Record a first demo (30s, one point: token change propagating)
- Start the weekly review ritual; establish the first WAD baseline
- Begin weekly research capture in `research/`
- Ship experiment 1 or 6 (CLI CTA placement, or copy-to-CLI on component pages)

**Realistic outcome:** a baseline exists, two articles are live, and we know
which platform docs people actually read.

## Days 31–60 — SEO and repeatable publishing

**Theme:** stop depending on posting cadence for traffic.

- Ship `/design-tokens/flutter` — fully, to the landing-page architecture
- Ship `/rtl-design-system`
- Campaign 2 ("Tokens without widgets") and Campaign 4 (RTL)
- Per-platform quick starts (experiment 11)
- First monthly review; drop the channel that is not working
- Enable GitHub Discussions if inbound justifies it

**Realistic outcome:** first organic search entries that are not brand queries;
publishing no longer the only traffic source.

## Days 61–90 — Amplification and retention

**Theme:** find out whether people come back.

- Campaign 3 ("One design language, native implementations")
- Evaluate Stage 3 launch criteria; execute if met
- Two or three more landing pages, based on observed search queries
- Retention analysis: do activated developers return?
- Decide `/blog`: only if ≥3 off-site articles have landed
- Prepare, do not submit, Product Hunt assets

**Realistic outcome:** a repeatable acquisition loop, a retention read, and an
evidence-based decision on whether a milestone launch is worth spending.

## Standing constraints

- No paid acquisition. Organic technical credibility first — paid before
  activation is understood just buys noise.
- No KinetixUI Pro marketing. Core adoption is the objective; Pro signals come
  from watching what Core users hit limits on.
- No auto-publishing. Drafts here, humans post.
- Every number re-derived at the moment of publication.
