Add seven business components (beta, React): `AmountDisplay` (a formatted amount with a spoken sign, tone independent of sign, tabular figures and its own bidi run), `GuestPicker` (several counts in a popover of NumberInput steppers with per-type and total limits), `CountrySelect` (a searchable country combobox over a list you supply), `PhoneInput` (a calling-code select and a tel field in one left-to-right control, with a raw value and no bundled numbering data or validation), `DateRangeInput` (a start and end calendar date as ISO strings, so no time zone can move them), `TimeSlotPicker` (a radio group of time slots with unavailable and limited states and a stated time zone) and `AddressFields` (the fields of a postal address in the order and wording you supply, with autofill tokens). `Timeline` gains an optional per-item `status` with a distinct marker shape and spoken word for each, `aria-current="step"` and an optional action. `NumberInput` gains localisable stepper labels, keeps focus on a stepper at its bound (`aria-disabled` instead of `disabled`) and announces stepper changes. `Calendar` now mirrors its navigation chevrons inside `KinetixDirectionProvider` and uses logical classes. Native platforms get composition guidance; Angular is planned.
