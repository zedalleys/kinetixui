Add four transactional foundations (beta, React): `ResultState` (success, error, warning, pending, info and partial outcome screens with a named status mark, reference, details and actions), `ReferenceCode` (a selectable, copyable code that announces whether the copy worked and never reports a copy that did not happen), `PriceBreakdown` (subtotal, taxes, fees, discounts, credits and total as a description list, amounts supplied formatted and mirrored under RTL) and `PassCard` (passes, tickets, permits and vouchers, rendering a supplied barcode or QR code without encoding it). Native platforms get composition guidance; Angular is planned.
