Kinetic Weave colour additions. The generated output gains the `navy` (50–950), `indigo` (50–900), `cyan` (100/400/700) and `violet` (100/400/600) ramps and a `--blue-75` step — 25 new CSS custom properties and 5 new keys on the token object, across the web, iOS, Android and Flutter outputs. Nothing is renamed or removed, so every existing token keeps its name and its meaning.
